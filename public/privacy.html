Privacy Policy

Effective October 16, 2026

Bark & Branch Landscape is a landscaping company in Orange County, California. This page explains what barkandbranchlandscape.com collects, how we use it and the choices you have. If anything here is unclear, call or text Palmer at 657-525-9737 or email palmer@barkandbranchlandscape.com.

What we collect

  • Quote form. Your name and phone number, plus anything else you choose to give us: email, city, the service you need and what you tell us about your project.
  • Chat. The chat's answers are automatic and run in your browser. Nothing you type there reaches us unless you type a phone number. When you do, we get your name (if you gave it), your number and the last part of the chat, so Palmer knows what you asked.
  • How you found us. If you came to the site through one of our ads or tagged links, the page notes the ad, campaign and search words from the link and adds them to your request. The note stays in that browser tab and is gone when you close it.
  • Visits. Google Analytics records how the site is used: pages viewed, device and browser, roughly where you are (city level), how you found the site, and actions such as sending the form or tapping our phone number. Our web host, Vercel, keeps standard records of requests to the site, including IP addresses, to run and protect it.
  • Broker accounts. Brokers who sign in get a cookie that keeps them signed in for 30 days. Quotes sent through the broker estimate builder reach us the same way the quote form does.

How we use it

  • To get back to you about your project: to call, text or email you, set up a visit and send your estimate.
  • To do the work if you hire us, and to keep our job, warranty and tax records.
  • To learn which pages, ads and searches bring people to us, so we stop paying for ads that don't help.

We don't sell your information, and we don't give it to other companies for their own marketing.

Calls and text messages

If you give us your phone number through this site, we may call or text you about your request and your project, for example to answer your questions, set up a visit or update you on the work. How many messages you get depends on your project. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any text and we'll stop texting you, or reply HELP for help. To stop calls, just tell us.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text-message opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

Who sees it

Palmer and the people who work with us on your project. We also use a few companies to run the business. They handle your information only to provide their service to us:

  • Formspree receives the quote form, chat call-back requests and broker quotes, and emails them to us.
  • Google runs our email, Google Analytics, the site's fonts and our Google ads.
  • Vercel hosts this website.
  • Telegram sends Palmer an alert on his phone when a request comes in.
  • Our phone and text-message provider carries our calls and texts.

The broker estimate builder also loads code from public libraries (jsDelivr and cdnjs), which see your IP address the way any website does. We may share information if the law requires it, to protect people or property, or if the business is sold.

Cookies

Cookies are small files a website keeps in your browser.

  • Google Analytics uses cookies to count visits and to tell new visitors from returning ones.
  • Brokers who sign in get a cookie that keeps them signed in.
  • The note about which ad or link brought you is kept in your browser tab, not in a cookie, and is cleared when you close the tab.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser's settings. The site works without them, except the broker sign-in. You can also turn off Google Analytics with Google's opt-out add-on, and read how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Tracking on other websites

Google can collect information about your visit here and your activity on other websites over time, under Google's own privacy policy. You can manage the ads Google shows you at My Ad Center. We don't let any other company collect information about your activity on this site.

Do Not Track

Some browsers can send a "Do Not Track" signal. There's no agreed standard for what a website should do with it, so this site works the same either way. To limit tracking, block cookies or use Google's opt-out add-on above.

Your choices

  • To see, correct or delete the information we have about you, call, text or email Palmer and we'll take care of it. We may keep what the law requires us to keep, such as contracts and invoices for work we did.
  • To stop texts, reply STOP. To stop calls or emails, tell us.

Keeping it safe

Your information is kept in password-protected accounts and seen only by the people who need it for your project. No website or email is perfectly secure, so please don't send card numbers, bank details or Social Security numbers through this site.

How long we keep it

We keep requests as long as they're useful for answering you and following up, and job records as long as we need them for warranty, tax and legal reasons.

Children

This site is for adults planning work on their property. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you think a child has sent us information, tell us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version on this page and change the date at the top.
