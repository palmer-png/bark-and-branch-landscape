    Terms

    Deposit

    10% of the contract price or $1,000, whichever is less, on approval. It reserves the crane date. Balance due on completion, 5-day terms.

    Hard digging

    Rock or very hard soil is $87.36 per hour per person. We tell the client before we proceed.

    Watering

    Sprinklers must be working the week before planting, or the tree needs hand watering bi-weekly until established.

    Crane standby

    If the tree delivery is late, standby is billed at the crane company's hourly rate.

    Not included

    Excess soil export, tree inspection drains, staking or anchoring, irrigation to the tree, crane permits, no-parking and traffic control, unless selected as extras.

    Gas surcharge

    Figured from the mileage between Santa Ana 92705 and the job, and added on the final invoice.

    48" box weight

    Instant prices cover 48" box trees under 2,500 lb. Heavier trees are quoted.

    Single large tree

    One 48" box tree is $4,900 and one 60" box tree is $8,500 to cover mobilization, unless the rest of the job comes to $1,500 or more. Then the regular price applies.

    Job minimum

    Every job is at least $2,000. Smaller jobs show a minimum job charge to make up the difference.

    Over 36 feet

    Hand carry and crane reach past 36 feet from the street are priced by Bark & Branch from the site map.
