Hedge Varieties We Offer

Every property is different so we carry five proven species that cover the full range of what Orange County homeowners need. Whether you want total privacy fast or a formal low border, one of these will be the right fit.

Ficus Nitida
Most Popular

The go-to privacy hedge in Southern California. Ficus nitida grows fast, stays dense and handles the heat extremely well. It forms a thick wall of glossy dark green leaves that can reach 20 to 30 feet if you let it run. Perfect for full privacy along property lines and great for blocking noise and wind too. Requires occasional pruning to keep its shape but is otherwise very low maintenance.

Height: 15 to 30 ft Full Sun Fast Growing Evergreen
Boxwood
Classic Choice

Boxwood is the classic formal hedge shrub and for good reason. It takes shaping extremely well which makes it ideal for clean, tidy borders and low hedges in front yards and entries. It is slow growing so it does not need much pruning once it is established. The Green Beauty variety is especially well suited to OC because it handles heat, humidity and drought better than most.

Height: 3 to 6 ft Sun to Part Shade Slow Growing Evergreen
Cherry Laurel
Dense & Flowering

Cherry laurel is one of the best options when you want a tall, dense hedge that also looks beautiful. It produces fragrant white flower clusters in spring and grows into a full, lush screen with glossy evergreen foliage. It fills in quickly and handles pruning very well so you can keep it exactly the height you want. A great choice for side yards and back fences where full coverage is the goal.

Height: 6 to 10 ft Part Shade to Sun Medium Growth Evergreen
Ligustrum
Low Maintenance

Ligustrum, also called Wax Leaf Privet, is one of the most adaptable hedge plants you can choose. It has dense glossy foliage, tolerates both full sun and partial shade and produces fragrant white flowers in spring. It grows to a solid mid-range height which makes it ideal for side yards, property lines and anywhere you need a clean, manageable screen without a lot of work.

Height: 8 to 15 ft Sun to Part Shade Medium Growth Evergreen
Indian Hawthorn
Low Border

Indian hawthorn is a compact, tidy shrub that works beautifully as a low border hedge or a foundation planting along the front of a home. It is beloved in warm climates for its dense growth, spring flowers and year-round good looks. Very low maintenance and drought tolerant once established. A great choice if you want something polished and neat without much upkeep.

Height: 2 to 4 ft Full Sun Slow Growing Evergreen

Not sure which one is right for your yard? We will help you figure that out during your free consultation.
